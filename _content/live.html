Live clan tracker

The live tracker follows one Pet Simulator 99 clan in real time. The big counter rolls up as battle points come in, the graph draws the last minutes of progress, and the badges show the clan's rank by points and by diamonds.

What you see on the tracker

  • Total points: the clan's battle score. New points are spread over the next seconds so the counter rolls instead of jumping.
  • Rank badges: place on the battle leaderboard (star) and on the diamonds leaderboard (gem).
  • Diamonds, average and members: deposited diamonds, points per member and the current member count.
  • Points graph: the score over the time the page has been open. A steeper line means a faster pace.

To track a clan, open its profile from Top Clans and press Watch Live. The address looks like /live/?name=TAG, so you can bookmark it or share it.

Live tracker FAQ

How often does it update?

The clan is fetched about every 10 seconds, and the active battle and diamond rank are checked every few minutes. The graph keeps the most recent points while the tab is open.

Can I put this on my stream?

Use the OBS overlays instead. They are transparent, sized for streams and update by themselves.
