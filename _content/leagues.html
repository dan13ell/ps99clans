League leaderboard

Pet Simulator 99 league standings

Leagues are a team leaderboard that BIG Games runs next to the classic clan battles. This page lists every ranked league with its total league points and member count, refreshed about once a minute from the official API.

How to use the Leagues page

  1. Browse the ranking. Leagues are shown 100 per page, ordered by points. Use the arrows under the list to move between pages. The page number shows where you are.
  2. Search by name. Type part of a league name into the search bar. The results keep each league's real position, so a league found on page 7 still shows its true rank.
  3. Open a league. Click a league to open its profile. You'll see the league's place, total points, member count out of capacity and a card for every member with the points they contributed.

Leagues vs. clans

A clan is the permanent group you join in Pet Simulator 99. It has a leader, officers, upgrades and a diamond bank, and it competes in every clan battle.

A league is a team ranking that is tied to its own events and seasons. The rules for earning league points are set per event, so a league's score only means something compared to other leagues in the same season. That is why this site keeps leagues on their own leaderboard instead of mixing them with clans.

What the numbers tell you

  • Points are the league's total score in the current league season.
  • Members show how full the league is. A league close to capacity with high points is usually very active.
  • Position is the place on the official leaderboard at the last refresh. Reward tiers depend on the final position at the end of the season.

Leagues FAQ

Why can't I find my league?

The search only covers leagues on the official leaderboard. A league with no points in the current season, or one ranked below the pages the API publishes, won't appear until it scores.

How often do league points update?

The full league list is refreshed about once a minute. A single league profile asks the API for fresh data each time you open it.

Are league points the same as clan battle points?

No. The official API tracks them as separate scores for separate competitions, each with its own leaderboard and rewards. Compare clans with clans and leagues with leagues.
