Clan profile: members, battle points and diamonds

The window above shows one Pet Simulator 99 clan in detail: its icon, country, description, total battle points, place and deposited diamonds, the last shout from its leaders and a card for every member.

How to read a clan profile

  • Battle points (star): the clan's total in the active clan battle. Between battles this shows the last battle's result.
  • Diamonds (gem): everything members have deposited into the clan bank. Clans spend these on upgrades and extra member slots.
  • Place: the clan's position on the battle leaderboard, or its overall position by points when no battle is running.
  • Members: the current member count out of the clan's capacity (up to 50).
  • Status: the latest clan shout, with who posted it and when. Leaders often use it for battle instructions or recruiting.

The member list is ordered the way the game orders it: the Leader first, then Officers, then everyone else. Each card shows the member's battle points and the diamonds they deposited, so you can see at a glance who is carrying the clan. Click a member to open their player profile.

Watch the clan live

The Watch Live button opens the live tracker. Points roll up as they arrive, there is a live graph of the last minutes, and you see the clan's rank by points and by diamonds. Leave it open on a second screen during the final hours of a battle.

Checking a clan before you join

A profile tells you a lot about a clan: how many members actually score, whether the officers are active, and if the clan's shout was updated recently. The find-a-clan guide shows what to look for.

Clan profile FAQ

A member shows 0 points. Are they inactive?

Not always. They may have joined after the battle started, play a different part of the game, or simply not have played the event yet. Look at the same member again later in the battle before drawing conclusions.

Why does a member show "@..." or "@Player_123"?

Usernames are looked up from Roblox separately. "@..." means the lookup is still running. "@Player_" plus a number means Roblox didn't return a name, for example for a deleted account.

How do I link to a clan?

Copy the address from your browser. It looks like ps99clans.site/clan-detail/?name=TAG and always opens the latest data for that clan.
