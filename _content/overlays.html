For streamers

Free Pet Simulator 99 overlays for OBS and Streamlabs

Show your clan's live battle points on stream without screen-capturing a website. Every overlay is a transparent browser source that updates by itself, so viewers see the numbers roll up while you play.

The five overlay types

Clan points

Your clan's icon, tag, total battle points, current place and points per hour, with a green +points bubble every time the score goes up.

Versus

Two clans side by side with a tug-of-war bar and the live point difference. Made for close races.

Top clans

A mini leaderboard. With your clan set, it shows the clans around you with real places, and cards slide when someone overtakes.

Player points

Your own battle points by Roblox username, with your clan tag and points per hour. Good for solo grinding streams.

Points goal

A progress bar toward a target such as "10B for the clan". It turns gold with confetti when the goal is reached.

Styles

PS99, Dark, Glass, Neon and Text-only looks, any accent colour, size and alignment. All previewed live before you copy the link.

Set it up in under a minute

  1. Choose an overlay type in section 1 at the top of the page and enter your clan tag (or username).
  2. Pick a look in section 2. The preview updates immediately. Turn parts like the flag or the +points bubbles on and off.
  3. Press Copy link in section 3. Note the Width and Height shown under the link.
  4. In OBS, add a Browser source, paste the link as the URL and set the width and height. In Streamlabs, use Browser Source.
  5. Drag it where you want it on your scene. That's it. It refreshes itself every 15 seconds.

There is a longer walkthrough with screenshots-in-words, troubleshooting and layout tips in the OBS overlay guide.

Overlay FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. The overlays are web pages that OBS and Streamlabs load with their built-in browser source. There is no plugin, download or account.

The background isn't transparent. What do I do?

The page itself is already transparent. Remove any custom CSS you typed into the browser source settings, and make sure you pasted the overlay link that the builder copied, not the link of the builder page.

Text looks blurry on stream.

Use the Size slider in the builder instead of stretching the source in OBS, and set the browser source width and height to the numbers the builder shows. Then the overlay is drawn at full sharpness.

It stopped updating.

Right-click the source in OBS and choose Refresh. If the clan tag was changed in game, create a new link with the new tag.

Do the overlays show ads?

No. The overlay pages never contain ads or anything else besides your stats, so nothing unexpected can appear on your stream.
