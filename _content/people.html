The game only shows you the players inside your own clan. This leaderboard puts players from every ranked clan on one list, so you can see who is earning the most battle points in the whole game and where you rank among them.

How the player ranking is built

There is no official "all players" leaderboard for clan battles, so PS99 Clans builds one. A background job walks through the clan leaderboard all day, 200 clans every minute. For every clan it reads the battle contribution of each member and stores the highest value it has seen. Players are then sorted by those points, and the top 25,000 are kept.

  • Rank is your position among all collected players, not just inside your clan.
  • Clan is the clan you are earning points for. If you leave or get kicked, your points stay (the game keeps them too) and the badge changes to No clan once you haven't been seen in any clan for about an hour.
  • Names are your Roblox username, looked up from your user ID. A brand-new player can show without a name for a few minutes.
TipClick any player to open their profile with an hourly points chart for the last 24 hours, their best hour, their average per hour and the players right above and below them.

Searching

The search bar accepts three things:

  • a Roblox username or part of it (an exact match is shown first),
  • a clan tag, which lists that clan's members in global order,
  • a numeric Roblox user ID.

Display names don't work. Use the @username from your Roblox profile.

What a good score looks like

Point values change completely from one battle to the next. In one battle a single action is worth 1 point, in another it is worth thousands. Don't compare your total with an older battle. Compare it with players near you right now.

The stats guide explains how to read points per hour and why the top of the list moves in bursts.

Top Players FAQ

Why am I not on the list?

Only the top 25,000 players with battle points are stored. You also need to be in a clan that is on the clan leaderboard. A player in a very small clan can be missed until the clan scores enough to rank. Earn a few more points and check again after the next collection cycle.

My points here are lower than in the game. Why?

Every clan is re-read once per cycle, which takes a few minutes. Your number is from the last time your clan was read. It catches up on the next pass.

Does the list reset?

Yes, when a new clan battle starts. Until then the final results of the last battle stay visible, so you can still check how you finished.
