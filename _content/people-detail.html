Player profile

Player stats for the current clan battle

The window above shows one player's clan battle performance: total battle points, global rank among the top 25,000, the clan they earn for, and an hourly chart of the last 24 hours of the battle.

What each stat means

  • Total points: everything the player has contributed to their clan in this battle.
  • Global rank: their position across all clans, plus the share of tracked players ahead of and behind them.
  • Hourly points chart: how many points they earned in each hour. Tall bars are grinding sessions, gaps are breaks.
  • Best hour: the highest single hour so far. A quick way to compare peak effort between players.
  • Average / hour: the points earned across the hours on the chart, divided by the number of hours. It is drawn as the dashed line.
  • Latest hour: points in the current (unfinished) hour, so this number grows while the player is online.
  • Neighbours: the players directly above and below on the global ranking, with the gap to each. Click one to jump to their profile.
NoteThe hourly chart starts when the site began tracking the battle. If tracking started part-way through, the first hours are missing rather than shown as zero.

Player profile FAQ

Why does it say the player is not in the top 25,000?

Only the 25,000 highest-scoring players of the current battle are tracked. Earn more points and the profile appears after the next collection cycle, which takes a few minutes.

Why does the clan badge say "No clan"?

The player hasn't been seen in any clan's member list for about an hour. Their points stay because the game keeps them, and the badge shows their last known clan when you hover it.

How do I find my own profile?

Search your Roblox username on People and click your card. Bookmark the page to come back to it. The link contains your user ID.
