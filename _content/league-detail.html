League profile: members and league points

This page shows one Pet Simulator 99 league: its icon, total league points, place on the league leaderboard and every member with the points they contributed in the current league season.

How to read a league profile

  • Points: the league's total score in the current season.
  • Place: the league's position on the full league leaderboard, worked out from all ranked leagues.
  • Members: the current member count out of the league's capacity.
  • Member cards: the leader first, then officers, then the other members, each with the league points they earned.

The page refreshes in the background while it is open, so you can keep it open during an event and watch your teammates' points grow. Click Leagues in the menu to go back to the full leaderboard.

League profile FAQ

Why does the place say "N/A"?

The place comes from the full league leaderboard. "N/A" means the league wasn't found in the ranked part of the list yet, usually because it has no points this season.

Is a league the same as my clan?

No. Leagues have their own membership and their own score. See the Leagues page for how the two differ, or the glossary for quick definitions.
