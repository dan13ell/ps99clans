Pet Simulator 99 clans explained

A clan is a team of up to 75 players with a shared tag, icon, diamond bank and upgrades. Clans compete in every clan battle, and joining an active one is one of the best ways to get limited pets. This guide covers how clans are organised and what every number on a clan profile means.

Creating a clan

To found a clan you need a Clan Ticket. It can be bought in the Exclusive Shop for Robux, and tickets have also appeared from the Index Merchant and in trading. Using the ticket creates the clan at level 1, with a small number of member slots and one officer slot. You then choose a short tag (the name shown in brackets, like [UN0]), an icon, a description and optionally a country.

Starting a clan is easy. Keeping one competitive is the hard part, because upgrades and extra slots cost a lot of diamonds. Most players get more out of joining an established clan first and learning how it is run.

Roles: leader, officers and members

RoleHow manyWhat they can do
Leader1Everything: invite and kick, appoint officers, change the icon and description, buy upgrades and slots, disband the clan.
OfficerStarts at 1, more can be boughtInvite and kick members (but never the leader) and post a clan shout.
MemberUp to 75 in totalEarn battle points and deposit diamonds.

If a leader wants to step down, leadership can be handed to an officer. The transfer takes seven days and can be cancelled in that time, which protects clans from someone taking over by surprise.

The clan shout is the message at the top of the clan window. On PS99 Clans it is shown as the status on a clan profile, with who posted it and how long ago. A recent shout is a good sign that the leaders are active.

The clan bank and diamond deposits

Members can deposit diamonds into the clan bank, with a minimum of 10,000 and a maximum of 1 billion per deposit. Deposited diamonds pay for clan upgrades and new slots. They also count towards each member's standing: within the same role, members are listed by how much they have deposited.

The total in the bank is shown as diamonds on our clan pages, and you can rank clans by it on Top Clans with the gem toggle. A high diamond total usually means a clan has been organised for a long time, even if it isn't winning the current battle.

Levelling a clan unlocks permanent bonuses for every member. The costs grow quickly:

The bonuses help with everyday farming as well as battles, so a higher level makes a clan more attractive to recruits. Values can be rebalanced in updates. The in-game upgrade menu shows the current numbers.

Member and officer slots

A new clan starts with only a few member slots. Each extra slot is bought with diamonds and gets more expensive as you go: early slots cost around 100,000 diamonds, and the last ones before the cap of 75 cost over a million each. Officer slots are much more expensive, rising into the hundreds of millions for the last ones.

That's why the member count on a clan card, like 47/75, is worth a look. The second number is the clan's capacity. A clan at 75/75 has invested heavily, while a clan with free slots may be recruiting.

Reading a clan on PS99 Clans

Every clan has a profile page on this site. Open it from Top Clans or by going to /clan-detail/?name=TAG. You'll find:

  • the clan's icon, country flag, description and latest shout,
  • its battle points, place and deposited diamonds,
  • every member in game order (leader, officers, members) with their battle points and diamonds,
  • a Watch Live button for the real-time tracker.
