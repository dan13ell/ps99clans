How to add live PS99 clan points to OBS and Streamlabs

Viewers love watching a number go up. A live clan points overlay turns a quiet grinding stream into a race your chat can follow. This guide walks through creating an overlay on PS99 Clans, adding it to OBS Studio or Streamlabs, and fixing the common problems.

  2. Build your overlay

1. Pick the right overlay

OverlayBest for
Clan PointsEvery clan battle stream. Shows total points, place and points per hour.
VersusClose races with one rival. The tug-of-war bar makes the gap easy to read at a glance.
Top ClansShowing where your clan sits among its neighbours, with animated overtakes.
Player PointsSolo grinding. Shows your own contribution by username.
Points GoalStream goals like "help the clan reach 10B tonight". Celebrates when reached.

2. Build your overlay

  1. Open the OBS Overlays page and choose a type at the top.
  2. Enter your clan tag. For Versus also enter the rival's tag. For Player Points enter your Roblox username, and the builder looks up your user ID for you.
  3. Choose a look. PS99 matches the game, Dark and Glass work over busy gameplay, Neon stands out, and Text only has no card at all.
  4. Pick an accent colour, the size and the alignment, and switch off any parts you don't need (flag, place, +points bubbles…).
  5. Check the preview. You can switch its background between the game, dark, green and none to see how it will look over your stream.
  6. Press Copy link. Under the link you'll see the recommended Width and Height.

Your settings are saved in your browser, so next time the builder opens exactly where you left it.

3. Add it to OBS Studio

  1. In the Sources panel click + and choose Browser.
  2. Name it, for example "Clan points", and press OK.
  3. Paste the link into URL.
  4. Set Width and Height to the numbers from the builder.
  5. Leave Custom CSS empty, or delete what OBS puts there by default if anything looks off. The overlay is already transparent.
  6. Press OK and drag the source into place.

4. Add it to Streamlabs

  1. Click + in the Sources list and pick Browser Source.
  2. Paste the link as the URL and enter the width and height from the builder.
  3. Confirm and position it on your scene.

The same link works in any software with a browser source, including vMix and XSplit.

5. Layout tips

  • Corners are your friend. The top corners usually have the least game UI in Pet Simulator 99. Keep the bottom free for your hotbar and chat.
  • Use the Size slider, not the OBS handles. Scaling in the builder keeps text sharp, while stretching in OBS makes it blurry.
  • Match alignment to position. Right-aligned overlays grow to the left as numbers get longer, so they never run off the screen on the right edge.
  • One overlay is often enough. Clan Points plus an occasional Versus during a close race beats a screen full of widgets.
  • Goals drive chat. A Points Goal with a round target gives viewers a reason to stay until it's reached.

6. Troubleshooting

The overlay shows "Loading" forever

Check the clan tag for typos. It has to be the clan's exact tag. Then right-click the source and press Refresh.

The background is white or black

Make sure you pasted the overlay link that the builder copied, which starts with ps99clans.site/overlay/, not the link of the builder page itself. Clear the Custom CSS field.

The numbers are not moving

Outside an active battle there is nothing to count. Check the countdown on Top Clans. During a battle the overlay updates every 15 seconds and spreads each increase smoothly over the next seconds.

It's cut off at the edge

Increase the browser source width and height a little, or lower the Size slider in the builder and copy the new link.
