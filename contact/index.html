Reporting a problem

The more detail, the faster it gets fixed. Please include:

  • the page address (for example /clan-detail/?name=TAG),
  • what you expected and what you saw,
  • your device and browser (for example "iPhone, Safari" or "Windows, Chrome"),
  • for OBS overlays: your OBS or Streamlabs version and the overlay link.

Wrong numbers?

All game data comes from the official PS99 API, and it can lag a minute or two behind the game at busy moments. If a number is still wrong after a few minutes, tell us which clan or player it is and what the game shows. A screenshot helps a lot.

Things we can't help with

PS99 Clans is an independent fan site. We can't help with Roblox accounts, purchases, bans or in-game items. Please contact Roblox support or BIG Games for those.
