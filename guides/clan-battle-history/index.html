Pet Simulator 99 clan battle history

Updated Oct 29, 20264 min readBy the PS99 Clans team

Since the end of 2023, almost every big Pet Simulator 99 event has come with a clan battle. This page collects the battles from the first Festive battle through the end of 2025: when each ran, which clan finished first and what earned points.

What the history shows

A few patterns stand out:

  • Dynasties happen. GOOP dominated much of 2024, and UN0 won a long run of battles in the first half of 2025. The two clans are tied on 12 first places each. Once a clan wins regularly, it attracts the strongest players, which makes the next win more likely.
  • But no clan wins everything. fr3e, BOSS, IZK, SOPU and others have all broken those streaks. A battle whose activity suits a different playstyle, or a well-timed final push, can be enough.
  • Battles got shorter. Early battles often ran two or three weeks. Through 2025 most ran about a week, which puts more weight on the opening days and the final push.
  • The scoring keeps changing. Hatching, collecting, minigames and "biggest catch" style records all appear. A clan that only knows one style can struggle when the format changes.
