PS99 clan glossary

Updated Oct 29, 20263 min readBy PS99 Clans

Clan chat is full of short words: "push at 8", "we're 2B behind", "min 50M". Here is what the common Pet Simulator 99 clan terms mean, and where you'll find each of them on this site.

Clans

Clan

A team of up to 50 players with a shared tag, icon, bank and upgrades. See clans explained.

Clan tag

The short name in brackets, like [UN0]. It identifies the clan everywhere, including in links on this site such as /clan-detail/?name=UN0.

Leader

The one player with full control of the clan: invites, kicks, officers, upgrades, icon and description.

Officer

A trusted member who can invite and kick members and post the clan shout. Officer slots are bought with diamonds.

Shout / status

The message at the top of the clan window, used for instructions and rules. Clan profiles here show it with the author and time.

Capacity

How many members a clan can hold right now, for example the 50 in "42/50". It grows when the clan buys member slots.

Deposit / clan bank

Diamonds members give to the clan. They pay for upgrades and slots and are shown with the gem icon.

Clan level

Upgrades bought with deposited diamonds that give every member bonuses like extra drop rate or walkspeed.

Country / flag

An optional country a clan can show. Top Clans can filter by it.

Clan battles

Clan battle

A timed competition, usually about a week, where clans earn points from one event activity. See how clan battles work.

Battle points

The score in the current battle, shown with the star icon. It resets when a new battle begins.

Contribution

The battle points one member earned for their clan. Shown on every member card and on Top Players.

Placement / place

A clan's position on the battle leaderboard. Final placement decides rewards.

Reward tier

A range of places that gets the same rewards, such as 2nd – 3rd or 11th – 50th. The border between two tiers is where the fiercest races happen.

Medal

A permanent gold, silver or bronze award a clan earns for a top finish in a battle.

Minimum / min

A clan's own rule for how many points each member should earn per battle, like "min 50M".

Kick limit

The game's limit on how many players a clan can kick per day during a battle, to stop member cycling.

Racing terms

Gap

The point difference between two clans. Compare shows it live.

Pace

How fast points come in, usually per hour. Faster pace beats a bigger lead over time.

Push

A planned window where many members play at once, often with boosts, to gain ground quickly.

Overtake

When one clan passes another on the leaderboard. Animated live on the Live Top 500.

Carry

A player who earns a large share of their clan's points. The top member cards on a clan profile show who is carrying.

Avg / person

Clan points divided by members. Shows how active a typical member is.

Leagues and the site

League

A separate team ranking with its own seasons and score. See the Leagues page.

Live tracker

The real-time view of one clan, opened with Watch Live on a clan profile.

Overlay

A transparent stat box for OBS or Streamlabs. Create one on OBS Overlays.

Public API

The official data service BIG Games provides for community tools. Every number on this site comes from it.
