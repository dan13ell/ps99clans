Clan battle strategy for leaders and members

Updated Oct 29, 20264 min readBy PS99 Clans

Most clan battles aren't won by the clan with the most dedicated player. They're won by the clan where the most members keep playing every day, and that saves its strongest push for the right moment. These habits help a clan finish higher than its size suggests.

Win the first hours

Each battle uses a different activity, so the first job is to work out the fastest way to earn points. In the first hours:

  • Test the main methods and compare points per minute. Is it better to hatch in the newest area or the cheapest one? Does a minigame reward speed or survival?
  • Post the best method in the clan shout and in your Discord so every member uses it, not just the experts.
  • Check which boosts, potions or items increase battle points, and agree when the clan will use them.

A clan that settles on the best method on day one can finish several places higher than an identical clan that works it out on day three.

Breadth beats peaks

Open your clan's profile and count the members with points. Raising that number is usually the cheapest gain available. Twenty extra members who each play for 30 minutes a day add more than one player grinding all night, and they don't burn out.

  • Set a small daily goal rather than one big battle minimum. "Play 20 minutes every day" keeps people involved better than "500M by Sunday".
  • Celebrate progress in public: top daily contributors, members who beat their own best hour (shown on each player profile).
  • Fill empty slots early. A slot that stays empty for the whole battle is lost points.

Know your real rivals

You aren't competing with the whole leaderboard, only with the clans right around a reward border. If the silver tier ends at 3rd and you're 5th, the clans in 3rd and 4th are the ones that matter.

  1. Find the reward border closest to you on Top Clans.
  2. Open Compare with your clan on the left and the rival on the right.
  3. Leave it running for an hour. If the difference line moves towards zero, you're faster. Divide the gap by the hourly change to estimate when you'd overtake.
  4. Repeat at different times of day. A rival from another time zone may be fast at night and slow in your afternoon, and that tells you when your pushes hit hardest.

Timing pushes and boosts

A push is a planned window where as many members as possible play at once, often with boosts active. Two things decide whether it works:

  • Timing. Pushing while a rival is asleep wins visible ground and can discourage them. Pushing right before the end leaves no time to react, but if it fails, there's no second chance.
  • Coordination. Announce the time a day in advance and remind people an hour before. A push with half the clan is a normal hour with extra stress.

Watch the result live. The rolling counter on the live tracker and the +points popups on the overlays make a push much more motivating for the members taking part.

The final day

The last 24 hours decide most tiers. Clans that held boosts back use them now, and gaps that stayed stable for days can close in an hour.

  • Know the exact end time. The countdown on Top Clans is taken from the official schedule.
  • Keep a buffer. Being 1% ahead an hour before the end is not safe. Rivals push too.
  • Watch the border live on the Live Top 500, where overtakes are animated as they happen.
  • Plan your last push to end a little before the deadline. Last-second points are a gamble, and leaderboards everywhere, including this one, need a moment to show the final standings.

Tips for members

  • Play a little every day instead of all at once. Your clan sees steady progress, and you don't burn out.
  • Check your profile on Top Players: the hourly chart shows which sessions actually paid off.
  • Follow your clan's method and boost timing, even if your own idea seems faster. Coordination wins battles.
  • Deposit spare diamonds between battles. Upgrades help everyone farm faster next time.
  • Play fair. Macros and exploits can get accounts punished and clans removed from the leaderboard.

After the battle

The final results stay visible on this site until the next battle starts. Use that window to review: who contributed, which push worked, at what hours your rivals were fastest. Clans that review every battle get better at the next one, and that, more than anything, is how mid-table clans climb into the medal tiers.
