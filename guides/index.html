About these guides

The guides are written and kept up to date by the people who build PS99 Clans. We play Pet Simulator 99 ourselves and watch the clan leaderboards every day. Game facts are checked against in-game information and community sources, and each guide shows the date it was last updated. When an update changes how clans or battles work, we revise the affected guides. If you spot something outdated, please tell us.
