About PS99 Clans

Welcome to PS99 Clans Leaderboards!

PS99 Clans is the ultimate community dashboard built specifically for Pet Simulator 99 players on Roblox. We provide live statistics, clan battle rankings, and top player performance trackers so you never miss a beat during competitive clan wars.

Key Features & Functionality

  • Clan Rankings: Real-time tracking of top-performing clans participating in current Clan Battles.
  • Top Players: Individual player performance search with live Roblox avatars, war points, and clan affiliations.
  • League Stats: Detailed breakdown of clan tiers, points required, and leaderboard status.
  • Fast Search: Instantly find your favorite players or clans across our optimized API infrastructure.

How Clan Battles Work in PS99

Pet Simulator 99 Clan Wars feature timed events where players collaborate with clan members to complete quests, mine diamonds, hatch rare pets, or conquer minigames. Points earned directly contribute to the overall clan placement on the leaderboards.
