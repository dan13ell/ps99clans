What's new on PS99 Clans

PS99 Clans is updated all the time, often several times a week during a clan battle. Here are the most notable changes, newest first.

October 29, 2026

  • Guides section. New in-depth guides on clan battles, clans, finding a clan, battle strategy, reading the stats, OBS overlays, the battle history and a glossary.
  • Help on every page. Each tool now has an explanation, tips and an FAQ below the main window.
  • Much more reliable profile pictures on clan, league and player pages. Avatars are cached in your browser, loaded in batches and retried automatically when Roblox is slow.
  • New About, Contact, Terms and an updated Privacy policy.

October 28, 2026

  • OBS overlays. A new overlay builder with five overlay types (Clan Points, Versus, Top Clans, Player Points, Points Goal), five looks, accent colours, size and alignment, a live preview and one-click copy for OBS and Streamlabs.
  • Game-style +points bubbles, animated overtakes in the Top Clans overlay and a celebration when a points goal is reached.

October 24 – 26, 2026

  • Live Top 500 rebuilt: a smoother points engine, cards that fill the screen, medal colours for the top places and a better layout on phones.
  • Player pages now show No clan for players who have left every clan, while keeping their battle points.
  • Hourly charts on player pages use "hours ago" labels.
  • Smoother touch scrolling in long lists and lazy-loaded clan icons.
  • Fixed zoom and vertical centring on mobile, so every page fits the screen the same way.

October 22 – 24, 2026

  • Compare. A new head-to-head page for two clans with rolling totals, the live point difference, diamonds, average per member and an interactive difference chart.
  • Hotbar. A game-style button bar under the main window for quick access to Clans, Compare, Live Top 500 and OBS Overlays.

Earlier

  • The core site: the top 1000 clans with country filter, leagues, the top 25,000 players leaderboard, clan, league and player profiles, and the live clan tracker.

Have an idea for the next update? Send it in. Many of the changes above started as player suggestions.
