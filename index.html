Live Pet Simulator 99 clan leaderboards

PS99 Clans tracks every clan battle in Pet Simulator 99 while it happens. Look up any clan, see who is carrying it, follow the race at the top of the leaderboard and check how far your clan is from the next medal tier, all in a layout that looks like the game itself.

The menu above works just like the in-game clan window. Top Clans lists the 1,000 highest-scoring clans, Leagues shows the league leaderboard and People ranks individual players by the points they earned for their clan. The buttons under the window open the extra tools: Compare, Live Top 500 and OBS Overlays.

    Where the numbers come from

    All clan, league and battle data comes from the official Pet Simulator 99 public API that BIG Games provides for community projects. Usernames and profile pictures come from Roblox. PS99 Clans doesn't change these numbers. It collects them, keeps short histories and presents them in a way that is easier to read than raw data:

    • Clan pages and the live tracker refresh roughly every 10 to 15 seconds.
    • Leaderboards (top clans, leagues) refresh about once a minute.
    • The player leaderboard is rebuilt continuously. A background job reads 200 clans every minute and stores each member's battle points, so every ranked clan is re-checked every few minutes.
    • Rolling numbers: when points jump, the counters roll up smoothly over the next few seconds instead of jumping, which is why a number can look like it is still "catching up".

    If something looks wrong, it is usually a short delay in the official API. Give it a minute. If it stays wrong, tell us.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is PS99 Clans official?

    No. PS99 Clans is an independent fan project. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by BIG Games or Roblox. It uses the public API that BIG Games offers so that the community can build tools like this one.

    How do I find my clan?

    Open Top Clans and type your clan tag into the search bar at the top of the window. Clicking a clan opens its profile with every member, their battle points and the diamonds they deposited.

    How do I find my own stats as a player?

    Go to People and search your Roblox username (not your display name). You will see your rank among the top 25,000 players of the current battle. Open your card for an hourly points chart, your best hour and the players just above and below you.

    Why is my clan not on the list?

    Top Clans shows the 1,000 clans with the most battle points. A clan with no points in the current battle, or one ranked below 1,000, won't be listed. You can still open any clan directly with a link like /clan-detail/?name=TAG.

    How often is the data updated?

    Clan profiles and the live tracker refresh every 10 to 15 seconds. Leaderboards refresh about once a minute. The official API itself can lag by a minute or two during busy moments, especially at the very end of a battle.

    Does it work on mobile?

    Yes. The whole window scales down to fit your screen, and all the tools work with touch. On very small phones, landscape mode gives you a bigger window.

    Is it free?

    Yes, every page and tool is free and needs no account. The site is paid for by a small amount of advertising.
