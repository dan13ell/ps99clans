Privacy policy

This policy explains what information PS99 Clans (ps99clans.site) collects, why, who it is shared with and the choices you have. The short version: we don't ask for your name, email or Roblox login, and you can use every page without an account.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Who we are

PS99 Clans is an independent fan website about Pet Simulator 99, run by a private individual. For privacy questions or requests, email danielmairich@gmail.com.

2. Information we don't collect

We don't offer accounts, and we never ask for your password, Roblox login, payment details or other sensitive information. Usernames, avatars and game statistics shown on the site are public game data from the official Pet Simulator 99 API and Roblox. They are not collected from you.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Server and network logs. Our hosting (GitHub Pages) and our data server (Cloudflare) process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, so the site can be delivered and protected from abuse. We don't use these logs to identify visitors.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are used and how the site performs, for example how many people visit Top Clans during a battle. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies, and the information is processed by Google.
  • Advertising. See section 4.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which pay for hosting and development. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's advertising policies.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used, through a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy settings link shown by that message.

The OBS overlay pages (/overlay/) never show ads.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Besides the advertising and analytics cookies described above, the site itself only uses your browser's local storage for convenience features. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

Stored itemPurpose
Overlay builder settingsRemembers the overlay you were designing.
Avatar cacheStores links to Roblox profile pictures for a few days so pages load faster.
Clan description cacheKeeps clan descriptions on Top Clans so the list loads faster.
Display preferencesRemembers choices like ranking by points or diamonds and a hidden navigation bar.

You can delete cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings. The site keeps working without them.

6. Third-party services

To work, pages load content from these providers, which receive your IP address as part of the request: Google (Fonts, Analytics, AdSense), Cloudflare (data server, cdnjs), jsDelivr (chart library), Roblox (avatars and usernames) and BIG Games (game data via our server). Links to other websites, such as our Google Forms feedback form, are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

7. Children's privacy

Pet Simulator 99 is played by people of all ages. PS99 Clans is a general-audience website. It doesn't ask visitors for personal information and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Parents can use the opt-out links above to turn off personalised ads. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold almost no personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled with the opt-out tools above or directly with Google. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

9. Data retention

We don't keep personal profiles of visitors. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them. Public game statistics (such as a player's battle points) are kept until the next clan battle starts and then replaced.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted on the What's new page.

11. Disclaimer

PS99 Clans is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Roblox Corporation or BIG Games. All game names, images and trademarks belong to their respective owners. See also our terms of use.
